Hi Cate,

I just listened to the [Next City episode](https://nextcity.org/podcast/how-we-build-community-ownership-and-self-determination) AmbitioUS made on community ownership and self-determination, and I'm still thinking about The Guild's land trust in Atlanta. Communities owning the land *and* deciding what happens on it? That's the whole ballgame for us. I HAVE to meet the team behind this.

Could we connect over Zoom for 25 minutes in the next couple of weeks?

A little about me: I am the Executive Director of [Architecture + Advocacy](https://architectureandadvocacy.org), a youth-led nonprofit in South LA and Flatbush, Brooklyn. We work at the intersection of community organizing and architecture (hello, culture!!) so communities of color have real self-determination over what gets built in their neighborhoods. Art and creativity is our entry point to civic power: residents imagine the future of their own blocks, and then we build it with them.

When a community group owns the land, we help them build on the spaces they already have. When they don't (yet!), we work with the architect/developer to make sure residents still shape what gets built.

I know AmbitioUS is set to sunset in 2028-- is there still time to partner with you all?

You can book a time that works for your schedule here: https://calendar.app.google/w1SbkYs4j4b8Q42H7

If none of those work, let me know and we will figure out something that does.
