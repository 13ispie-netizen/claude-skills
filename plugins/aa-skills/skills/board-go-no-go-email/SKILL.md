# Board Go/No-Go Email

Drafts the email Erin sends the A+A board to get a go/no-go vote before applying to an RFP/RFSQ/grant. Follow the template and rules below exactly.

---

## EMAIL RULES (always)

**Universal:**
1. The ask lands within the **first 3 lines**.
2. Cut the fluff. Be direct. Every word earns its place.
3. **Hyperlink** the necessary documents (link the doc names inline; never paste raw URLs).
4. Voice: warm, direct, high-energy, CAPS for emphasis where natural, signs `-E`. No emojis.

**Board-specific:**
1. Subject line starts with the action in brackets: `[vote]`, `[read]`, `[resource]`, etc. For this email it is `[vote]`.
2. The ask is **always this exact sentence**: "Please respond with your go/no-go, reasons for NOT applying, and/or request for a meeting by [one week from today]."

---

## TEMPLATE

Fill the brackets. Keep it this short. The three documents are always hyperlinked (link the go/no-go docs produced by the set-up-rfp / rfp-quick-facts skills).

> **Subject:** [vote] [Opportunity short name]
>
> Hi All,
>
> [Issuer] just launched its "[opportunity]", due [deadline]. [One line on the specific fit + who is interested, e.g. "There is a specific engagement category, and [name(s)] and I are interested in applying."] Please respond with your go/no-go, reasons for NOT applying, and/or request for a meeting by [one week from today].
>
> [1-2 sentences framing what selection does and does NOT guarantee. For a bench/pre-qualification: it does not guarantee any projects; it short-lists A+A to apply for future projects, decided project-by-project on mission fit.]
>
> - [Project Quick Facts]
> - [Gut Check]
> - [project folder, including full RFP/RFQ]
>
> [Optional, only if relevant: if board members are traveling, offer to extend the deadline so there is time to put together a strong application.]
>
> -E

---

## NOTES

- Compute "[one week from today]" from the actual current date; state it as a weekday + date (e.g. "Wed 08/03").
- The three bullets are the Quick Facts sheet, the Gut Check, and the project folder (which contains the full RFP) - all hyperlinked. Pull these from the project's go/no-go documents folder.
- Present the draft in chat for Erin's review. Only save to Gmail drafts or send once she confirms recipients (the board list / group alias).
- Reference example (the LA County Bench RFSQ ask) is the canonical version of this template.
